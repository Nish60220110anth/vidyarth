1. Adobe at a Glance

  • Founded: 1982; HQ in San José, CA
  • Core Businesses: Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator), Document Cloud (Acrobat, Sign), Experience Cloud (marketing, analytics)
  • Culture Pillars: “Genuine, Exceptional, Innovative, Involved”


2. Typical Interview Process

  1. Application & Resume Screen
  2. Recruiter Phone Screen (30 min)
    • Fit questions: why Adobe, career goals
  4. Technical/Functional Phone Screen (45–60 min)
    • Engineers: data structures & algorithms
    • PM/Design: case scenarios, portfolio walk-through
  6. On-Site / Virtual Loop (3–5 interviews)
    • Coding & System Design (for engineers)
    • Behavioral (STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result)
    • Role-Specific Deep Dives (product sense, UX critique, marketing strategy)
  8. HR & Compensation Chat


3. Core Competencies Tested

  • Engineering:
    • Algorithms (trees, graphs, DP)
    • Scalable-system design (“design Dropbox at scale”)
  • Product/Design:
    • Product sense (“improve Adobe XD”)
    • UX critique (“evaluate this interface”)
  • Behavioral:
    • Team-collaboration stories
    • Conflict-resolution examples
