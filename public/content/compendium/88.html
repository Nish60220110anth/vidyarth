About the Company

  • ARGA is a valuation-based manager of global equity strategies, with investment professionals based in developed and emerging markets in the US and India.
  • ARGA manages equity portfolios on behalf of clients. Presently offering Global, International (ex-US) and Emerging Market Equity Strategies.
  • They believe emotions impair investment decisions, which is ably supported by the field of Behavioural Finance. It is these impairments that ARGA seeks to quantify in order to identify investment opportunities.
