1. About the Firm

Samagra is a mission-driven governance consulting firm that partners with senior political and bureaucratic leadership of Indian states to address governance challenges at scale. Their engagements are designed as systemic transformation programs that usually span 2–3 years, moving from problem diagnosis to roadmap design and implementation.

  • Headquarters: Delhi, India
  • Founded: 2012
  • Unique Proposition: Blend of management consulting discipline and grassroots understanding of governance ecosystems, powered by data and technology.
  • Core Objective: To make governments more accountable, efficient, and citizen-centric.


2. Approach

Samagra’s problem-solving philosophy integrates:

  1. Top-Down Consulting Rigor: Structured problem-solving, frameworks, and diagnostic tools akin to leading management consultancies.
  2. Bottom-Up Ground Realities: Deep on-ground understanding of the governance ecosystem, ensuring solutions are practical and context-specific.
  3. Technology & Data Leverage: Use of digital dashboards, analytics, and govtech platforms to drive implementation with minimum delays and maximum efficiency.

This hybrid model ensures reforms are not just conceptual strategies but implemented transformations.


3. Focus Sectors & Key Work

Agriculture

  • KONNECT – With Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation (BMGF) and Government of Odisha to improve farmers’ income.
  • ADAPT – Partnership with Odisha and BMGF to increase agricultural output and farmers’ income.
  • ADAPT-F&ARD – Extends ADAPT to Fisheries & Animal Husbandry.
  • Krishi SAMRIDHI – With BMGF and Government of Uttar Pradesh to provide officers real-time visibility on scheme implementation.
  • LEAP – Livestock Expansion & Advancement Program in Odisha, focusing on Dairy and Goatery value chains.

Education

  • Mission Prerna (UP) – Foundational literacy & numeracy (FLN) for all primary students by 2023.
  • Saksham Haryana (Education) – With CM’s Office & Education Department to make 80% of students grade-competent.
  • Samarth (HP) – Statewide transformation of government schools for better learning outcomes.
  • NIPUN Bharat (UP) – Ensure all primary school students achieve FLN by 2026.

Health

  • STRIDE (Odisha) – Improve tribal health & nutrition outcomes with BMGF and Piramal Foundation.
  • Niramaya (UP) – Reform nursing and paramedical training (over 4.4 lakh professionals).
  • Health Diagnosis & Design (Gujarat & Odisha) – Six-month initiative to enhance accessibility, quality, and speed of public healthcare delivery.

Fellowships

  • CMGGA (Haryana) – Chief Minister’s Good Governance Associates programme, embedding young professionals to catalyse flagship program delivery.

Service Delivery

  • Antyodaya Saral (Haryana) – Online public service delivery platform for citizen–government interaction.
  • Family ID (UP) – Unified family database linked to all citizen-facing schemes and services.

Skills & Employment

  • Saksham Haryana (Skills & Employment) – Partnering with Employment and Skill Development Departments to transform skilling and job ecosystems.
  • Skill India – Designed implementation roadmap for MSDE Vision 2025.
  • Young Warrior NXT – Life skills program for 14–18-year-olds, promoting future readiness and emotional resilience.


4. SamagraX – GovTech Vision

  • Vision: To create an OpenSource “government operating system.”
  • Approach: Develop reusable, sector-specific govtech products using OpenSource components, scalable across different states.
    This reflects Samagra’s pivot to scalable technology-enabled governance.


5. Governance Frameworks

Samagra has codified its learnings into frameworks that are often referenced in interviews:

  1. Governance Matrix: Evaluates a government’s readiness for reform based on political salience (will) and system capacity (people, processes, infrastructure).
  2. Panchsutras for Governance Transformation: Five principles for successful systemic reform at any government level.
