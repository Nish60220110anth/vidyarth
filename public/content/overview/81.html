About the Company


Founded in 2002 by Aman and Swati Sachdeva, Synergy Consulting, Inc. is an independent global company that provides financial and corporate advisory services for a wide range of infrastructure projects. With its headquarters in the USA (Virginia), the firm has expanded its presence to offices in over a dozen countries, including the UK, India, UAE, South Korea, Japan, and Saudi Arabia. Synergy Consulting partners with both public and private sector clients, financial institutions, and other stakeholders to structure and close large, complex transactions in sectors such as power (conventional and renewable), transport, water, and social infrastructure.

Key Services


Synergy Consulting offers a comprehensive range of financial advisory services throughout the entire project lifecycle.

  • Project Finance & Bid Advisory: Includes project feasibility analysis, financial structuring, financial modeling, and debt raising/syndication.
  • Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A): Provides both buy-side and sell-side advisory, including due diligence, valuations, and assistance with transaction documents.
  • Financial Modeling & Audit: Develops and audits complex financial models for project finance, M&A, and business planning.
  • ESG & Circular Economy: Offers ESG assessments, sustainability strategies, and advice on circular economy principles to minimize environmental impact.
  • Development Advisory: Provides expert technical and commercial advisory services from pre-feasibility to implementation and operations of infrastructure and utility projects.


Key Values

  • Excellence: Continuously improving and delivering the highest quality services.
  • Collaboration / Together: Working closely with clients and partners, and respecting diverse perspectives.
  • Courage: Thinking and acting boldly, and being open to new ideas.
  • For Better / Sustainability: Taking a long-term view and being committed to responsible business practices that contribute to the well-being of communities.
